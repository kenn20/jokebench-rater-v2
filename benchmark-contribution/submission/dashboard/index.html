02 / WHAT THIS DOES & DOES NOT SAY

Evidence with the edges visible.

Interpretation guardrails

  • Small, single-repeat pilot. 70 distinct public tasks, reused across arms. 560 records are not 560 independent questions.
  • No universal uplift claim. Mixed healthcare changes; programming sits at the reported scoring ceiling.
  • Humor is not human-validated. A model judge supplies a stand-in 1–5 rating. Desired 50% and 70% funny-rate coverage remains unmet.
  • Recorded evidence, not provider verification. Arithmetic can be checked from artifacts; model execution, identity and generated-code scoring have separate verification limits.
  • Public-benchmark contamination is possible. Tiny subsets and an unreviewed execution harness limit generalization. Not clinical guidance or a safety certification.
